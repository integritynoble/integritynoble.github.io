Abraham (Chengshuai) Yang

Research Director and Cofounder, NextGen PlatformAI · PhD

I measure what AI agents can really do, and I build agents that do science.

I designed SI-Level, a measure of how much verified work an AI agent delivers, where a result it claims but cannot back up counts against it. Before AI agents, I spent ten years on machine learning for imaging: compressed ultrafast photography, deep unfolding and plug-and-play reconstruction, and generative and vision-language models.

Current work

Two connected projects: a neutral measure of AI agents, and a library of real scientific checks that agents can be measured on.

AI evaluation

SI-Level

A cumulative scale for a model plus its agent harness. Fresh hidden tasks, sealed answer keys and checkers outside the agent's reach; false claims count against the score.

si-level.platformai.org →
AI for science

Physics World Model (PWM)

A library of verified imaging and physics problems with independent judges on real data, used to test whether AI agents produce results that hold up.
