Research

Abraham (Chengshuai) Yang · Research Director and Cofounder, NextGen PlatformAI · PhD

I work on one question: when an AI system says it finished a piece of work, is the work actually done and correct? I build the measurement for that (SI-Level), and a library of real scientific problems where the answer is checked by independent judges against real data. Before this, I spent ten years on machine learning for computational imaging, from building ultrafast cameras to deep reconstruction networks, diffusion priors and vision-language models.

Research directions

AI evaluation

SI-Level

A cumulative scale for what a model plus its agent harness reliably delivers. Fresh tasks from generators, sealed answer keys, typed validators outside the agent's write access, and a rule that a claimed but unproven result counts against the score. Paper under review.

si-level.platformai.org →
Agents for science

Verified scientific results

AI agents build and run scientific methods (imaging, low-dose CT, cryo-EM); independent judges score them on held-out real data, with pass thresholds fixed before scoring. Audits catch results an agent graded itself.

physicsworldmodel.org →
Imaging ML

Computational imaging

Compressed ultrafast photography, deep unfolding and plug-and-play reconstruction for snapshot compressive imaging, diffusion and GAN priors for inverse problems, and InverseNet, a benchmark of reconstruction methods under operator mismatch (under review).
